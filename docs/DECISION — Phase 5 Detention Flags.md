## §1 — Authorization to retain counsel — APPROACH APPROVED; wording DRAFT-until-Taylor-signs

- **Narrow scope:** receive information + hire counsel only. Not a financial POA, not a G-28.
- **Notarization:** offer, don't require — "sign now, notarize if you can." Never blocks someone who can't reach a notary in time.
- **Add an optional named-attorney slot:** default stays general ("hire an attorney"), plus an optional "if I have already chosen an attorney: [name / firm]" line — a pre-named attorney who has agreed is the strongest version and ties to the pre-signed G-28 (§3).
- **Set realistic expectations in the surrounding copy:** this is the **bridge to counsel, not a substitute for the G-28.** ICE/EOIR may still require their own forms before releasing case information to a non-attorney; the binder must not imply the trusted person can compel an agency to talk to them on the strength of this page alone.
- **Binder-only document — never carried.** The card bears only the "I have a signed authorization" checkbox (consistent with §2).
- This is the one piece with operative legal effect for a layperson; final language carries DRAFT-until-Taylor-signs.

## §2 — Choice-mark — LOCKED

Refusal script on the carried **Pocket Card**; the fight-vs-depart **decision-mark on the Binder Page only.** A seized card must never carry a stated intent. No further input needed.

## §3 — Blank G-28 — APPROACH: optional, opt-in, not auto-bundled; SHIP/VETO is Taylor's

- Include the fillable blank G-28 as an **explicit opt-in attachment** — **not** auto-bundled into the binder packet.
- **Label exactly:** "For your attorney to complete and sign once they agree to represent you. Do not file this yourself." This closes the real failure mode (a frightened person filing a G-28 naming a lawyer who hasn't agreed) and matches how the form works (it requires the attorney's own signature to be valid).
- **Instructions-and-link is the default path** for anyone who'd rather not carry it.
- **Taylor's liability call:** ship the fillable form or veto. The fillable attachment stays **disabled until he ratifies**; build the path, gate the enablement.

## §4 — EN microcopy — APPROVED IN SUBSTANCE; wording DRAFT-until-Taylor-signs

- Rights script, the DO-NOT-SIGN rule, the identity block with the exact-match helper line, and the honest voluntary-departure limits (doesn't erase the 3/10-year bars; missing the date turns it into a removal order) are accurate and on-voice.
- **Reconcile the voluntary-departure wording across card and binder** — the one clarification that prevents the most dangerous misread: *don't sign a voluntary-departure form an officer hands you; voluntary departure is something you **request from the judge**, with counsel.* The card's "don't sign … voluntary departure" and the binder's "ask the judge for voluntary departure" must be made explicitly non-contradictory.
