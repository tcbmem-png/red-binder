# **HOW TO SIGN YOUR POWER OF ATTORNEY**

This sheet explains how to sign your **{{governing_law_state}}** Durable Financial Power of Attorney so it is honored as widely as possible. Keep it with your binder. **It is not part of the Power of Attorney and grants nothing.**

---

## **The one rule that makes it travel**

Sign your Power of Attorney **in front of a notary public AND two adult witnesses**. Neither witness may be your Agent, and neither should be someone who benefits from your decisions. No U.S. state requires more than this — so signing this way clears every state, even if you move or your Agent has to act across state lines.

---

## **Steps**

1. **Print** the Power of Attorney. Do not sign it yet.
2. **Bring two witnesses** — adults who are not your Agent.
3. **Go to a notary.** A bank, a shipping store, many libraries, or a mobile notary can help.
4. **Sign in front of the notary and both witnesses.** Sign only there — not before.
5. **The witnesses sign** on the witness lines.
6. **The notary completes** the acknowledgment and applies their seal.
7. **Keep the signed original** in your Red Binder. Give a copy to your Agent and tell them where the original is.

---

## **Important**

A Power of Attorney does nothing until you sign it in front of a notary, following your state's rules. Printing it is the first step, not the last.
