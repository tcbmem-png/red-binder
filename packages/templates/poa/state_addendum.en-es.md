## **Why this page is here**

{{governing_law_state}} uses its own power-of-attorney law rather than the national Uniform Power of Attorney Act. Your Power of Attorney is written to be honored anywhere and is **governed by the law of {{governing_law_state}}** — the state where you live and where your Agent will use it.

Two things help it travel:

- **Your document already asks others to rely on it.** It states that any bank or person may, in good faith, rely on it, and it holds them harmless for doing so. That request is part of the document itself.
- **It is signed to the strongest standard** — a notary and two adult witnesses. See the execution-instructions sheet.
